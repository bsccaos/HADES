# Advanced Hardware Architectures Lab (HADES)

Welcome to the **HADES** research group documentation website. Our lab focuses on high-performance Systems-on-Chip (SoCs), novel hardware IP blocks, fault-tolerant architectures, and systems software design.

---

## 🚀 Key Research Areas

- **Systems-on-Chip (SoCs)**: Energy-efficient and fault-tolerant multi-core architectures.
- **Hardware IP Blocks**: Hardware accelerators, cryptographic units, and execution blocks.
- **Systems Software & Compilers**: Low-level drivers, toolchains, and hardware verification tools.
