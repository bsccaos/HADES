# HArdware Dependability for Embedded Systems (HADES)

Welcome to the official documentation website for the **HArdware Dependability for Embedded Systems (HADES)** research group at the [Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC-CNS)](https://www.bsc.es/).

HADES operates within the **High-Performance Embedded Systems (HPES)** laboratory in coordination with the [**SOftware Dependability for Embedded Systems (SODES)**](https://www.bsc.es/discover-bsc/organisation/research-structure/software-dependability-embedded-systems-sodes) and [**Applications for Intelligent Dependable Embedded Systems (AIDES)**](https://www.bsc.es/discover-bsc/organisation/research-structure/applications-intelligent-dependable-embedded-systems) research groups.

---

## 🎯 Mission & Overview

Modern high-performance embedded systems across automotive, avionics, and space industries are increasingly complex in both hardware and software. Across these domains, systems demand not only high performance, but also a strict set of non-functional properties related to **dependability, safety, security, and reliability**.

The HADES group develops novel hardware designs as well as their required low-level software support. Our designs range from Register Transfer Level (RTL) implementations for FPGAs and ASICs to emulation and simulation models.

The team consists of **more than 20 researchers**, including PhD students, engineers, and postdocs with established industrial and academic experience.

## 🔬 Core Research Lines

=== "⚡ Performance & WCET"
    - **Guaranteed Performance**: Facilitating Worst-Case Execution Time (WCET) computation and minimizing WCET bounds.
    - **Energy Efficiency**: Low power and energy consumption for battery-powered and space-constrained harsh environments.

=== "🛡️ Functional Safety"
    - **Safety Standards**: Compliance with **ISO 26262** (Automotive), **DO-254** (Avionics), and **ECSS** (Space).
    - **Architectural Primitives**: Diverse redundancy, timing predictability, reliability, and design-for-test.

=== "🔒 Security & Post-Quantum"
    - **Cybersecurity Primitives**: Ensuring data confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity.
    - **Post-Quantum Crypto**: Hardware support for next-generation post-quantum security algorithms.

=== "🛰️ Reliability & Radiation Testing"
    - **Harsh Environment Testing**: Fault injection, Single Event Effects (SEE) analysis, and beam irradiation (neutrons, protons, heavy ions).

=== "🏎️ Novel Architectures & Accelerators"
    - **Heterogeneous Compute**: Integrating GPUs, AI/ML/DL accelerators with time determinism and safety compliance.

=== "💻 Hardware-Software Co-Design"
    - **Low-Level Software**: Custom compilers, drivers, and runtime support to enable hardware acceleration features.
